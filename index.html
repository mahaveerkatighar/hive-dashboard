Wi-Fi Setup Mode

The command has been sent. The hive will leave your current Wi-Fi network, so this dashboard will stop receiving new data until setup is finished.

  1. Wait about 10 seconds for the device to restart its Wi-Fi.
  2. On your phone, connect to the network SMART-HIVE-SETUP.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1 in your browser.
  4. Scan for networks, enter the new SSID and password, then Save & Connect.
  5. The hive reconnects on its own and this dashboard resumes automatically.

If the new password is wrong, the device keeps its old settings and stays in setup mode so you can try again. It cannot lock itself out. If you lose contact entirely, hold the BOOT button on the ESP32-S3 for 3 seconds to re-enter this mode.
